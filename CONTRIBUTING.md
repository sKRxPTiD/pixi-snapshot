# Contributing

Keep PiXi useful offline, free of tracking, and independent of Google Play Services. Preserve the four main actions and make optional details available from dialogs. Use public, editable defaults rather than device-specific addresses or personal paths.

Describe the user-visible problem, your change, and the Android versions you checked. Run `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug :app:lintDebug` and the relevant checks in `docs/VALIDATION.md`. Changes to archives or transfers should cover malformed inputs, interrupted operations, and credential exclusion.

Contributions are made under the repository's MIT license. Do not submit proprietary app code, signing material, APK collections, personal backups, or credentials. The maintainer may review contributions as time allows; no support SLA is offered.
